All icons have their resolution increased from the original 64x64/32x32 to 256x256 (some sizes may be higher, Eg. SniperScopes). Icons that were squished to fit 64x64 aspect ratio are un-squished to their normal size (Eg. Shock Blaster/Disc Blade Gun from UYA).

Project started in 2024 working few icons per day for fun and learning to use Inkscape, until decided to recreate pretty much all of the icons. All icons are made by hand in Inkscape as accurately to the original icons as possible (within reason) close to their original colouring. No AI/Generative AI was used in the process of making the icons.

Up Your Arsenal and Deadlocked icons feature transparency, but the folders also include icon variants with no transparency.
Folders may also include original- and alternative variants of the icons, which are marked with the text "custom/alt" in their filename.
