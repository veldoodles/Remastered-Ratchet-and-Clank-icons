# Remastered-Ratchet-and-Clank-icons
Collection of various icons from PlayStation 2 Ratchet &amp; Clank-games, remastered in higher quality.

All icons have their resolution increased to 256x256. Icons that were squished to fit 64x64 aspect ratio are un-squished to their normal size (Eg. Shock Blaster/Disc Blade Gun from UYA).

Project started in 2024 working on few icons per day for fun until decided to recreate nearly all of them. All icons are made by hand in Inkscape as accurately to the original icons as possible (within reason) close to their original colouring. No AI/Generative AI was used in the process of making the icons.

Up Your Arsenal and Deadlocked icons feature transparency, but the folders also include icon variants with no transparency.
Folders may also include original- and alternative icons, but they are clearly marked with the text "custom/alt" in their filename.
